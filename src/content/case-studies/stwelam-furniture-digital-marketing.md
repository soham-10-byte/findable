## Executive Summary

**STWELAM Furniture** is a premier manufacturer and showroom provider of contemporary living, bedroom, and modular office furniture. Despite offering high-craftsmanship products and competitive pricing, their digital presence prior to partnering with Findable was limited, resulting in heavy reliance on walk-in foot traffic and word-of-mouth referrals.

Findable developed an integrated digital acquisition engine combining **custom showroom website development, Google Business Profile (GBP) local optimization, on-page SEO, and hyper-targeted performance marketing**. 

Within four months of execution, STWELAM Furniture scaled their digital pipeline to generate **over ₹15 lakh in monthly revenue** directly attributed to digital search and performance marketing channels.

---

## The Challenge

Before the campaign, STWELAM Furniture faced three critical operational bottlenecks:

1. **Invisible in High-Intent Local Searches:** Nearby homeowners and interior designers searching for *"custom modular sofa near me"* or *"luxury wooden dining table"* were finding competing retail chains.
2. **Outdated, Slow Website:** Their previous catalog website was unoptimized for mobile browsing, lacked pricing context, and suffered from slow image loading times exceeding 4.5 seconds.
3. **Inconsistent Lead Inflow:** Lead generation was volatile, with quiet weekday periods and zero predictable online consultation bookings.

### 1. High-Performance Showroom Website
We engineered a modern, ultra-responsive showroom website focused on visual clarity, tactile product imagery, and rapid inquiry paths:
* Built with clean semantic HTML and optimized WebP product galleries loading in under 1.1 seconds.
* Designed modular room-concept lookbooks that allow visitors to request customized dimensions and quotation requests in two clicks.
* Added clear WhatsApp Business and direct phone consultation triggers for immediate showroom booking.

### 2. Local SEO & Google Business Profile Domination
To capture high-intent buyers in the local metropolitan area:
* Standardized Name, Address, and Phone number (NAP) citations across verified trade and regional business directories.
* Optimized primary and secondary GBP categories, populated complete product collections with pricing ranges, and implemented weekly photo upload schedules.
* Implemented a structured review collection system that elevated their Google Maps rating to 4.9 stars with 180+ authentic customer reviews mentioning specific furniture pieces.

### 3. Full-Funnel Performance Marketing
To accelerate pipeline volume alongside organic search:
* Deployed targeted Google Search Ads capturing commercial intent terms (*"solid teak wood sofa set"*, *"modular office workstations"*).
* Ran Meta visual carousel and reel campaigns targeting local homeowners aged 28–55 within an 18-kilometer radius of the showroom.
* Structured retargeting audiences for website visitors who browsed living room or dining collections without booking a consultation.

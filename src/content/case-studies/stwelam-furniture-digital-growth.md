## Project overview

STWELAM Furniture partnered with Findable to strengthen its digital presence and generate business through a coordinated online growth programme. The engagement brought together website development, search visibility, local SEO, and performance marketing rather than treating each channel as a separate activity.

The reported outcome was more than **₹15 lakh in revenue generated** through the combined digital effort. The figure is presented as the overall reported result; the measurement period and attribution methodology should be confirmed with the client before publication as a time-bound or channel-specific claim.

## The growth challenge

For a furniture business, customers often research products and suppliers online before making an enquiry or visiting a store. A useful digital presence therefore needs to do more than display products: it should help people discover the business, understand its offering, and take the next step.

The brief for this engagement was to build that connected path from discovery to enquiry by improving the website, strengthening visibility in relevant searches, and supporting demand generation.

## Findable's approach

### 1. Website development

We worked on the brand's website as a central destination for its digital activity. The website development work supported the brand's online presentation and gave campaigns and search traffic a destination where prospective customers could learn more and make contact.

### 2. Search engine optimization

SEO work was part of the broader growth programme, with the aim of improving the website's visibility for relevant furniture-related searches and helping potential customers discover the business organically.

### 3. Local SEO optimization

Local SEO was included to strengthen discovery by people searching for furniture businesses in the relevant service area. This supported the connection between online search and local customer demand.

### 4. Performance marketing and digital marketing

Performance marketing and broader digital marketing complemented organic and local search activity. The combined approach helped the brand reach potential customers through multiple digital touchpoints instead of relying on a single acquisition channel.

## Reported outcome

The engagement generated a reported **₹15 lakh+ in revenue** for STWELAM Furniture through the combined work across digital marketing, SEO, local SEO, website development, and performance marketing.

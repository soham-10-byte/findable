## Executive Summary

**MAXLI Perfumes** is an artisanal, direct-to-consumer (D2C) fragrance brand crafting long-lasting luxury perfumes at accessible price points. Entering India's fiercely competitive fragrance and personal care market, MAXLI required more than conventional paid ads; they needed an authentic brand narrative, an authoritative online presence, and a multi-channel growth system.

Findable partnered with MAXLI Perfumes to engineer an omnichannel growth strategy covering **social media brand establishment, custom SEO website development, performance marketing, and offline pop-up integration**.

Within three months of launch, MAXLI Perfumes established steady customer acquisition, surpassing **₹2 lakh+ in monthly rolling revenue** with a compounding 32% repeat customer reorder rate.

---

## The Challenge

Selling luxury fragrances online presents unique hurdles:

1. **The "Smell Barrier":** Consumers cannot sample scents through digital screens, necessitating high brand trust, sensory copywriting, and compelling visual proof before purchase.
2. **Ad Fatigue & Rising CAC:** Relying exclusively on cold Instagram ads was driving Customer Acquisition Costs (CAC) to unsustainable levels without building organic brand equity.
3. **Fragmented Brand Touchpoints:** The brand lacked an authoritative digital home where customers could explore fragrance pyramids (top, heart, base notes) and read verified customer reviews.

### 1. Social Media Establishment & Visual Storytelling
We positioned MAXLI Perfumes as an aspirational yet approachable luxury lifestyle essential:
* Designed a cohesive aesthetic across Instagram, showcasing minimalist packaging, ingredient provenance, and daily wear longevity.
* Produced micro-content demonstrating olfactory notes (e.g., woody amber, fresh bergamot, smoky oud) and real wear-test benchmarks.
* Collaborated with micro-creators and lifestyle tastemakers to generate unboxing reels and authentic blind scent reactions, generating over 150,000 monthly organic impressions.

### 2. SEO & Brand Website Development
We engineered a clean, high-performance website tailored for fragrance discovery:
* Built sensory fragrance exploration pages detailing top, middle, and base notes with clear longevity ratings (8–12 hours).
* Structured on-page SEO targeting emerging search terms (*"long-lasting luxury perfumes under ₹1500"*, *"best woody citrus edp for men"*).
* Implemented review widgets with verified photo uploads to eliminate buyer hesitation.

### 3. Performance Marketing & Retargeting
* Launched targeted Meta conversion campaigns focused on discovery sample packs, dramatically lowering initial trial friction.
* Built automated post-purchase email and WhatsApp sequences offering exclusive discounts on full 100ml bottles to sample pack buyers.

### 4. Offline Brand Touchpoints & Pop-Ups
* Supported physical brand activations, flea market pop-ups, and luxury retail kiosk samplings.
* Distributed QR-code discovery cards at offline events, seamlessly driving physical testers to the digital storefront for seamless repeat online reordering.

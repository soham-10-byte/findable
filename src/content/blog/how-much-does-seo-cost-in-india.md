## The Reality of SEO Pricing in India

India has emerged as one of the world's most dynamic hubs for digital marketing and search engine optimization. However, pricing varies wildly across the market—from bargain freelancers offering "rank on page 1 for ₹4,999" to premier agencies delivering holistic **SEO, AEO (Answer Engine Optimization), and GEO (Generative Engine Optimization)** solutions at enterprise retainers.

Understanding what you are paying for—and the risks associated with rock-bottom pricing—is critical before signing an agency contract.

## What Determines the Cost of SEO?

No two websites require the exact same level of work. Four primary factors dictate the required investment:

1. **Current Domain Authority & Technical Health:** A brand-new domain requires entity establishment, schema architecture, and foundational link building from scratch. An established site with crawl errors needs surgical technical cleanup.
2. **Competition Level in Your Niche:** Ranking for a local term like *"best dermatologist in Kolkata"* requires significantly fewer resources than ranking globally for *"best CRM software"* or *"buy whey protein online"*.
3. **Content Production Quality:** Authentic, subject-matter-expert content optimized for both human readers and AI answer engines requires real research and editorial craft, rather than generic AI-generated regurgitation.
4. **Target Geography:** Ranking within a single Indian city requires local citations and local schema; ranking across the US, UK, and Europe requires international SEO (`hreflang`), international digital PR, and fast global CDNs.

## Beware of the "Cheap SEO" Trap (Under ₹10,000/mo)

Many business owners are lured by budget agencies advertising packages at ₹3,000 or ₹5,000 per month. Here is what typically happens behind the scenes of those cheap packages:

* **Spam Backlink Networks (PBNs):** Links placed on compromised sites, expired domains, or cheap link farms that trigger Google manual actions or algorithmic de-indexing.
* **Zero Technical Work:** They ignore core web vitals, structured schema markup, and crawl budgets because they lack developer resources.
* **Generic Automated Reports:** You receive auto-generated PDFs from free audit tools without actionable analysis or strategic guidance.

Repairing a domain penalized by black-hat techniques often costs 3x to 5x more than doing it right from day one.

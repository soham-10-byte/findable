## What is a Content Gap Analysis?

A **content gap analysis** is one of the highest-ROI activities in modern SEO. Instead of guessing what topics your audience might care about, you reverse-engineer the exact search terms and content architectures that are already generating thousands of qualified visitors for your top competitors.

By identifying the high-intent queries where your competitors rank on page 1 while your domain is either absent or ranking on page 3, you create a data-backed editorial roadmap that drives immediate business impact.

---

## The 3 Types of Content Gaps

```
1. Domain-Level Gaps (Entire topics/services you lack)
2. Page-Level Gaps (Specific questions/subheadings your ranking page missed)
3. Funnel-Level Gaps (Missing Top-of-Funnel vs. Bottom-of-Funnel intent pages)
```

1. **Domain-Level Gaps:** Keywords where 2 or 3 of your competitors rank in the top 10, but your website has no indexed URL at all.
2. **Page-Level Gaps:** Pages where you already rank on page 2 (positions 11–20), but competitors outperform you because their page includes crucial subheadings, FAQs, comparison tables, or code snippets that your page omits.
3. **Funnel-Stage Gaps:** Having plenty of educational blog posts (Top of Funnel) but missing high-intent comparison and pricing pages (Bottom of Funnel) like *"Competitor A vs Competitor B"* or *"Agency pricing guides"*.

## Step-by-Step Content Gap Framework

### Step 1: Identify Your True Search Competitors

Your direct business competitors are not always your search engine competitors. 

* **Business Competitors:** Companies offering identical services in your local market.
* **Search Competitors:** Domains that consistently occupy page 1 of Google for your target commercial and informational queries (often including publishers, aggregators, or software platforms).

Choose **3 to 5 domains** that match your core service offerings and target audience.

---

### Step 2: Run the Gap Comparison

Using your tool of choice (Ahrefs, SEMrush, or SE Ranking):

1. Enter your root domain in the target field.
2. Add your 3 main competitors in the comparative fields.
3. Apply filters:
   * **Set Search Volume:** Minimum 100+ searches/month.
   * **Set Ranking Positions:** Competitors rank in positions 1–10; you rank > 20 or not at all.
   * **Filter by Intent:** Prioritize *Commercial* and *Transactional* queries before addressing *Informational* queries.

```
Example Filter Rule:
[Competitor 1 Ranks 1-10] AND [Competitor 2 Ranks 1-10] AND [My Site Ranks > 20 OR Unranked]
```

---

### Step 3: Categorize Opportunities by Commercial Value

Not every keyword gap deserves a new 2,000-word article. Group your discovered gaps into a prioritized matrix:

| Priority | Gap Type | Example Query | Action Required |
|---|---|---|---|
| **P1: Immediate Revenue** | High-intent commercial queries | *"aeo agency pricing"*, *"shopify technical audit service"* | Create dedicated service or landing pages |
| **P2: High-Volume Informational** | Problem-solving guides & comparisons | *"how to fix faceted navigation in shopify"*, *"ahrefs vs semrush"* | Write in-depth long-form tutorials |
| **P3: Page-Level Expansion** | Missing subheadings on existing URLs | *"how long does an seo audit take"* | Update existing ranking article with a new H2 and FAQ |

---

### Step 4: Outperform the Existing Page 1 Ranking Assets

When you build content to fill a gap, do not merely duplicate what competitors have done. Apply the **Information Gain** principle:

* **Include Real Data & Benchmarks:** Provide proprietary survey findings, case study metrics, or actual pricing numbers.
* **Optimize for Answer Engines:** Position concise, direct answers within the first 50 words of each heading to win AI citations.
* **Incorporate Semantic Media:** Add original diagrams, tables, infographics, or code templates that competitors lack.
* **Connect Connected Schemas:** Implement `Article`, `FAQPage`, and author `Person` structured data.

---

### Step 5: Internal Linking & Authority Flow

Once your new page is published:
1. Search your site for relevant keywords using Google: `site:yourdomain.com "target keyword"`.
2. Add contextual internal links from those existing indexed pages pointing to your new post.
3. Submit the new URL for immediate indexing in Google Search Console.

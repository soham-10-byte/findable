## What Are the Differences Between SEO, AEO, and GEO?

**Search Engine Optimization (SEO)** drives rankings on search engine results pages; **Answer Engine Optimization (AEO)** extracts direct answers into featured snippets and AI Overviews; and **Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)** establishes brand authority so LLMs recommend and cite your business in synthesized answers.

While they operate on different delivery mechanisms, all three rely on the same fundamental core: high-performance technical architecture, undeniable entity clarity, and authoritative proof.

## Comparison Breakdown

| Dimension | SEO (Search Engine Optimization) | AEO (Answer Engine Optimization) | GEO (Generative Engine Optimization) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Primary Platform** | Google Search, Bing, DuckDuckGo | Google AI Overviews, Featured Snippets, Siri | ChatGPT Search, Perplexity, Claude, Gemini |
| **Output Type** | 10 blue links, local map packs | Direct answer box, snippet cards | Synthesized text answers with footnotes/citations |
| **Key Lever** | Keywords, backlinks, internal linking | Answer-first copy, schema, QA structure | Entity consensus, deep domain authority, static data |
| **User Action** | Click through to site | Read answer on SERP or click citation | Follow cited brand recommendation |

## Which Optimization Should You Prioritize First?

To maximize ROI without wasted effort, implement optimizations in this chronological order:

1. **Phase 1: Technical SEO Baseline:** If search engines cannot crawl your static HTML or pass Core Web Vitals, AI crawlers will fail as well. Ensure 200 HTTP status, canonical consistency, and clean sitemaps.
2. **Phase 2: AEO Content Structuring:** Restructure existing content so answers precede explanations. Convert ambiguous paragraphs into numbered lists and concise comparison tables.
3. **Phase 3: GEO Authority Building:** Build corroborating entity profiles across high-authority external platforms, earn verified customer reviews, and publish proprietary benchmarks.

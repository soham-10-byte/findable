## What is Generative Engine Optimization?

**Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)** is the process of structuring your digital footprint so large language models (LLMs) and generative search engines recommend, cite, and reference your brand. Unlike traditional search engines that rank links by PageRank, generative engines synthesize multimodal answers using retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) and pre-trained entity knowledge.

If a buyer asks ChatGPT or Perplexity "What is the best SEO agency for D2C brands in India?", GEO is the work that ensures your brand appears in the synthesized list with an active reference link.

## How Generative Engines Select Sources to Cite

Generative models rely on both training data and real-time search crawlers (such as OAI-SearchBot, PerplexityBot, and Googlebot). When generating answers, AI models prioritize:

- **Entity Consensus:** Corroborated facts across independent sources (industry directories, press mentions, review platforms, and structured schema).
- **Contextual Authority:** Deep, domain-specific teardowns containing original data, technical precision, and verifiable case studies.
- **Machine-Parsable Static Markup:** Clean HTML that RAG scrapers can ingest without relying on client-side JavaScript execution.

## The Three Pillars of GEO

```
1. Technical Foundation  -> Zero JS gating, clean semantic DOM, fast response
2. Information Architecture -> Unified @graph JSON-LD, entity linking, clear descriptors
3. Off-Site Consensus    -> Authoritative third-party citations, reviews, verified profiles
```

### 1. Technical Crawlability for AI Bots
Never block AI search bots in `robots.txt`. Bots like `OAI-SearchBot`, `PerplexityBot`, and `ClaudeBot` must have full access to your content. Do not hide primary text behind tabs or infinite scrolls that require JavaScript execution.

### 2. Entity Disambiguation
Ensure your brand's core entity identity is unambiguous. Maintain identical definitions of what you do, who you serve, and where you operate across your schema markup, your About page, and your social profiles.

### 3. Original Data and Proof Points
AI engines favor sources that provide primary evidence: benchmarks, original case studies, and exact step-by-step methodologies. Generic content is summarized into oblivion, but original data is quoted with attribution.
